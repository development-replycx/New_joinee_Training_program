How each day runs

Daily rhythm

  • Morning: concept session led by the trainer or subject expert.
  • Midday: live walkthrough on the platform or a real client account.
  • Afternoon: hands-on exercise, shadowing of live tickets or calls.
  • Last 15 minutes: recap, open questions, and the trainee writes down what they learned.

What the trainee builds

  • A personal runbook per product: flows, error codes, contacts, escalation steps.
  • A client and vendor sheet: who they are, what they use, who to call.
  • A list of ten issues seen in shadowing, with the fix and the client message that went with it.

Before day 1

  • Accounts and access for each platform, the ticketing tool and shared drives.
  • A named trainer or subject expert for each product block.
  • Sandbox or test accounts for WhatsApp, RCS, SMS, ReplyCX and email.
  • Names of the vendors and clients to be covered.
